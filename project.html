Project Overview

Project Overview Mobile phones have become an essential part of our daily lives, transforming the way we communicate, learn, work, and access information.

DigiLife – Mobile Technology in Everyday Life explores the various uses of mobile phones, their advantages and disadvantages, and their impact on students and society. This project aims to promote awareness about responsible mobile usage and encourage a healthy digital lifestyle.

Project Objectives

  • Understand the societal importance of mobile technology.
  • Explore various core applications across key life sectors.
  • Identify positive contributions alongside digital health concerns.
  • Analyze mobile device usage patterns among students.
  • Promote healthy and intentional mobile consumption habits.

Project Scope

This initiative examines mobile application ecosystems across primary verticals: education, communication, digital payments, healthcare access, productivity tools, and modern media entertainment.

Why DigiLife?

DigiLife was established to bridge digital awareness with practical habit design, offering students a structured resource to evaluate smartphone usage constructively.
