About Our Team

We are a team of students working together on the DigiLife project as part of our Community Service Project (CSP). Our team aims to create awareness about the importance of mobile technology in everyday life.

Through research, teamwork, and collaboration, we explored the different uses of mobile phones, their benefits, and the challenges of excessive usage. Each team member contributed ideas and efforts to make this project informative and useful.

Our goal is to encourage people to use mobile technology wisely and responsibly.
